E-Day: November 8, 2024

On 8 November 2024, we held e-Day, a Dutch e-value meet-up, at CWI (Science Park 123, Amsterdam). It was an informal meeting for researchers working on e-values, with talks, open-problem sessions and time for discussion.

The programme included talks, open-problem sessions and time for discussion, followed by drinks and dinner.

Registration is closed.
