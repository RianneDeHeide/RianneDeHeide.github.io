E-mailing list

e-mail

e-mail is a mailing list for concise announcements about meetings, positions and other news related to game-theoretic statistics, e-values and anytime-valid inference. It is hosted on the University of Twente list server.

Subscribe: send a blank e-mail (no body, signature or subject) to e-mail-subscribe-request@lists.utwente.nl. You will receive an e-mail with a link to confirm your subscription.

Post: subscribers can send announcements to e-mail@lists.utwente.nl.
