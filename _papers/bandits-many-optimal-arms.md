title: Bandits with many optimal arms
research_id: item-07
authors: Rianne de Heide, James Cheshire, Pierre Ménard and Alexandra Carpentier
publication_date: '2021'
about: This paper studies stochastic bandits with a possibly infinite collection of arms and a positive proportion of optimal arms. It characterises learning rates for both cumulative regret and best-arm identification and gives matching or near-matching algorithms.
abstract: We consider a stochastic bandit problem with a possibly infinite number of arms. We write p* for the proportion of optimal arms and Δ for the minimal mean gap between optimal and sub-optimal arms. We characterize optimal learning rates in both cumulative regret and best-arm identification in terms of the budget, p* and Δ. For cumulative regret we give a lower bound and a UCB-style algorithm with a matching upper bound up to logarithmic factors, and show that knowledge of p* is necessary for adaptation. For best-arm identification we give an exponential lower bound on the probability of outputting a sub-optimal arm and an elimination algorithm whose upper bound matches it up to a logarithmic factor in the exponent. The results also apply to related quantile and good-arm identification problems.
