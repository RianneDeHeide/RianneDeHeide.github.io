title: The truth-convergence of open-minded Bayesianism

authors: Tom F. Sterkenburg and Rianne de Heide
publication_date: '2022'
about: This paper studies an open-minded form of Bayesian learning in which new hypotheses may be introduced during the learning process. It shows that a natural existing proposal can lose the usual truth-convergence guarantee and develops a modification that restores a version of it.
abstract: Wenmackers and Romeijn formalize an open-minded Bayesian inductive logic that can dynamically incorporate statistical hypotheses proposed during the learning process. We show that their proposal does not preserve the classical Bayesian consistency guarantee of merger with the true hypothesis. We diagnose the problem and offer a forward-looking open-minded Bayesian approach that preserves a version of this guarantee.
