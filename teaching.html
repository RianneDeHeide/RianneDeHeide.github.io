Teaching

I teach statistics and machine learning at levels ranging from undergraduate service courses to specialised national Master's courses. My teaching includes mathematical statistics, statistical learning, Bayesian statistics, e-values, probability, and applications in the health sciences.

Slides, exercises and other resources are collected on the Teaching material page.
